7. sınıf · Tema 3 was / were Düzenli fiiller (-ed)

Üçüncü tema ile geçmiş zaman başlıyor. Yılın ilk iki teması bugünü anlatıyordu; buradan sonra dört tema boyunca geçmişte kalacaksın. Başlangıç noktası en kolay iki biçim: was / were ile geçmiş durum, düzenli fiillerin -ed hâli ile geçmişte tamamlanmış eylem. Üstüne 2. temadan gelen question tags, bu kez geçmiş zaman biçimiyle tekrar ediyor.

Temanın üç temel kalıbı:
GEÇMİŞ DURUM: I was at home yesterday. They were at the health centre. TAMAMLANMIŞ: She visited the doctor last week. We didn't walk to school yesterday. GEÇMİŞ TEYİT: You were tired, weren't you? He rested all day, didn't he?

3. was / were ile geçmiş durum

ÖzneOlumluOlumsuzSoru
I / he / she / itwaswasn'tWas I / he / she / it …?
you / we / theywereweren'tWere you / we / they …?
I was ill yesterday. My brother wasn't at school last Monday. We were in the garden all afternoon. Were you at the dentist? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. There was a long queue. / There were two doctors.

There was → tekil, There were → çoğul.

4. Düzenli fiiller: -ed hâli

OLUMLU: özne + V2 (-ed) She walked home. OLUMSUZ: özne + didn't + V1 She didn't walk home. SORU: Did + özne + V1 Did she walk home? CEVAP: Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
Yazım kuralıÖrnek
Genel kural: + -edwalk → walked, visit → visited, rest → rested
Sonu e ise: + -dlike → liked, dance → danced, smile → smiled
Ünsüz + y: -iedstudy → studied, cry → cried, carry → carried
Ünlü + y: + -edplay → played, enjoy → enjoyed, stay → stayed
Kısa fiilde son ünsüz ikizlenirstop → stopped, plan → planned, shop → shopped

Zaman sözleri (Simple Past işaretleri):

yesterday · last night · last week · last summer two days ago · an hour ago · in 2020 when I was a child · this morning (sabah bittiyse)

Bu sözlerden biri varsa fiil geçmiş hâlde olur. Zaman sözü, zamanı belirler.

En kritik kural: didn't ve Did gördüğün an fiil yalın hâlde (V1) kalır. "She didn't visited" yanlıştır; doğrusu "She didn't visit". "Did you walked?" yanlıştır; doğrusu "Did you walk?" Geçmişlik bilgisi zaten did'in içinde taşınıyor, iki kez işaretlenmez.
-ed nasıl okunur? Yazımı tek ama sesi üç türlüdür: walked ve stopped sonunda sert bir t duyulur; played ve cleaned sonunda yumuşak bir d duyulur; sonu t veya d ile biten fiillerde ise fazladan bir hece açılır: visited, rested, needed. Bu ayrımı kulakla yerleştir; fonetik sembol ezberlemene gerek yok.

6. Örnek diyalog

Nurse: You weren't at school yesterday, were you? Zeynep: No, I wasn't. I was at home. I had a bad headache. Nurse: Oh dear. Did you rest all day? Zeynep: Yes, I did. I stayed in bed and I didn't use my phone. Nurse: Good. Your screen time was very high last week, wasn't it? Zeynep: It was. My mother talked to me about it. Nurse: How was your throat this morning? Zeynep: Better, thanks. But my knee still hurts. I played basketball on Saturday. Nurse: You didn't warm up before the match, did you? Zeynep: No, I didn't.

Yapı kontrolü: "weren't … were you?" olumsuz cümle → olumlu tag. "was … wasn't it?" olumlu → olumsuz. "didn't … did you?" olumsuzda did. "Did you rest?" sorusunda fiil yalın. "stayed, talked, played" düzenli fiiller; "had" düzensiz — onu 4. temada öğreniyorsun.

9. Sık yapılan hatalar

  • didn't sonrası V2 kullanmak. "didn't visited" değil, "didn't visit".
  • Did ile soruda V2 kullanmak. "Did you walked?" değil, "Did you walk?"
  • was / were'ü özneye göre şaşırmak. "They was" değil, "They were".
  • was'lı cümleye did'li tag takmak. "He was ill, didn't he?" değil, "wasn't he?"
  • study → studyed yazmak. Ünsüz + y → studied.
  • "sore throat" yerine "throatache" demek. Böyle bir kelime yok.

10. Kendini test et

  1. "We ____ at the health centre yesterday." → was / were
  2. "She didn't ____ her medicine." → took / take
  3. "study" fiilinin geçmiş hâli? → studied / studyed
  4. "You were at home, ____?" → weren't you / didn't you
  5. "He watched a film last night, ____?" → wasn't he / didn't he
  6. "Diş ağrısı" hangisi? → toothache / backache
Cevapları göster
1) were (we → were) 2) take (didn't sonrası fiil yalın) 3) studied (ünsüz + y → -ied) 4) weren't you (cümlede were var → tag were ile) 5) didn't he (V2 var → tag did ile) 6) toothache (backache = sırt ağrısı)

Sık sorulan sorular

"There was" ile "There were" nasıl seçilir?

Arkasından gelen isme bakılır. Tekil isim geliyorsa There was ("There was a nurse"), çoğul isim geliyorsa There were ("There were three nurses"). Sayılamayan isimlerde de was kullanılır: "There was some water."

Düzenli ve düzensiz fiil ayrımı nerede öğretiliyor?

Bu temada yalnızca düzenli fiiller (-ed alanlar) veriliyor. go-went, have-had, see-saw gibi düzensiz fiiller 4. temaya bırakılmış. Bu yüzden 3. tema alıştırmalarında -ed eki her zaman işini görür.

Geçmiş zamanda soru sözcükleri nasıl kullanılır?

Soru sözcüğü en başa gelir, sonra normal soru düzeni kurulur: "Where were you yesterday?" · "Why did she stay at home?" · "How was your day?" Did'den sonra fiil yine yalın kalır.

Question tag'de özne "there" olursa ne yapılır?

there özne gibi davranır ve tag'de aynen tekrar edilir: "There was a long queue, wasn't there?" Zamire çevirmeye çalışmana gerek yok.
